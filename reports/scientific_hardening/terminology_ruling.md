# Index-cohort terminology (Phase 0.2)

## Index-grade composition (Verified, n=707 rows / 150 patients)
| Index grade | Rows | Patients | Positive rows | Positive patients | Positive rate (rows) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ID | 67 | 27 | 13 | 5 | 19.4% |
| LGD | 87 | 38 | 41 | 22 | 47.1% |
| NDBE | 553 | 137 | 53 | 23 | 9.6% |
| **Total** | 707 | 150 | 107 | 50 | 15.1% |

(ID = indefinite for dysplasia; NDBE = non-dysplastic Barrett's; LGD = low-grade dysplasia at index.)

## Terminology ruling (Verified reasoning)
- The cohort **includes 87 LGD rows / 38 patients at index** (positive rate 47%). It is **NOT dysplasia-free**.
- **"Before dysplasia is visible" is INACCURATE** and must not be used — index LGD cases are dysplastic at prediction time.
- **Scientifically correct phrase:** **"pre-high-grade"** / **"non-HGD/IMC index"** — excludes HGD/IMC/OAC at index (0 such rows) but includes NDBE, indefinite, LGD. "Pre-event" is acceptable shorthand for "before the LGD2+ event" if defined; "dysplasia-free" is wrong.
- **Recommended thesis wording:** *"a pre-high-grade surveillance cohort (non-dysplastic, indefinite, or low-grade Barrett's at the index biopsy; HGD/IMC/OAC excluded at index), predicting progression to the LGD2+ endpoint at the next biopsy."*

## Separate claims recommended
1. **NDBE + indefinite (620 rows, ~10-19% positive):** the genuine *early-detection* claim (future dysplasia from currently non-/indefinite-dysplastic tissue). Strongest, most novel framing.
2. **LGD index (87 rows / 38 patients, 47% positive):** *risk-stratification of existing LGD* (which LGD persists/progresses) — clinically distinct (LGD is already actionable).
3. **Full pre-high-grade cohort (707):** headline, but note the mixed composition and the LGD stratum's much higher base rate.
