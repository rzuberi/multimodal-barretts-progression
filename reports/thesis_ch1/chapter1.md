## 1.2 Cohort and endpoint definition

**Endpoint (locked).** The primary endpoint is `NextBiopsyProgression_LGD2plus`:
the next biopsy is HGD/IMC/OAC, or satisfies the second-consecutive-LGD rule
(operationally, HGD/IMC/OAC or two consecutive LGD biopsies). LGD3+ is retained
only as a supplementary/legacy endpoint.

**Cohort derivation.** The strict pre-event cohort is generated by
`src/barrett/data/pre_event.py`. A row is eligible only when the next-biopsy
LGD2+ endpoint is evaluable, the current biopsy is not already an LGD2+ event and
not after the patient's first LGD2+ event, the next biopsy is demonstrably later,
and both imaging and CNV are available.

![Figure 1.1 — Strict pre-event LGD2+ cohort derivation.](fig_cohort_flow.png)

**Figure 1.1.** Cohort flow. Of 959 eligible-schema rows (160 patients), the
strict pre-event cohort retains **707 rows / 359 biopsies / 150 patients**, of
which **107 rows / 50 patients** are LGD2+ positive. Exclusions: endpoint not
evaluable (38 rows), current biopsy already at an LGD2+ event (183), and rows
after the first LGD2+ event (31). *(Source: `lgd2_final_pre_event_cohort_flow.csv`.)*

## 1.3 Modalities and models

**[DRAFT]** Two input modalities:
- **Histopathology** — whole-slide images, tiled and encoded with the UNI2
  foundation model, aggregated per slide with attention-based multiple-instance
  learning (ABMIL).
- **Copy-number variation** — genome-windowed CNV features per sample.

**Fusion families evaluated.** CNV-only and image-only unimodal baselines; early,
intermediate, and co-attention fusion; and two late-fusion combiners (mean of
per-modality probabilities; stacked-logit). **[TODO]** one paragraph per family

## 1.4 Evaluation protocol

**[DRAFT]** All discrimination is reported at the **patient level** under **5-fold
patient-disjoint cross-validation** (no patient contributes to both train and
test). Operating-point metrics use a threshold cross-fitted on inner-validation
folds targeting high specificity. Uncertainty is quantified by patient-level
bootstrap (paired across models on the same resample). The primary comparison is
the multimodal model vs the CNV-only baseline on AUPRC.

### 1.5.2 Does histopathology add signal? (primary comparison)

![Figure 1.3 — Paired differences vs CNV-only baseline with 95% bootstrap CIs.](fig_paired_differences_forest.png)

**Figure 1.3.** Paired model−CNV differences (patient-level bootstrap, 95% CI).
For the headline late-mean model:
- **ΔAUPRC = +0.091 (95% CI −0.036 to +0.219)** — favours multimodal but the CI
  includes zero.
- **ΔROC AUC = +0.111 (95% CI +0.002 to +0.219)** — excludes zero.
- **ΔBrier = −0.032 (95% CI −0.062 to −0.004)** — excludes zero (lower is better).

*(Source: `lgd2_final_pre_event_paired_differences.csv`.)*

**[DRAFT] Interpretation.** On the prespecified primary metric (AUPRC), the
multimodal benefit is positive but not conclusive — the 95% CI crosses zero. The
two secondary discrimination/calibration metrics (ROC AUC and Brier) both favour
multimodal with CIs excluding zero. The honest reading is that **histopathology
adds a likely but not definitively conclusive signal on the primary metric, with
supportive secondary evidence** — not that multimodal is unambiguously superior.

### 1.5.3 Clinical operating points

![Figure 1.4 — Operating points at the cross-fitted threshold.](fig_operating_points.png)

**Figure 1.4.** At the cross-fitted threshold, CNV-only reaches 90% specificity
but only **28% sensitivity**; late mean holds 80% specificity at **58%
sensitivity** — i.e. the multimodal model detects roughly twice as many
progressors at a modest specificity cost. *(Source:
`lgd2_final_pre_event_model_comparison.csv`.)*

## 1.6 Interpretability

**1. CNV gene/window importance for LGD2+.** The final `cnv_only` estimator is a
scikit-learn pipeline (median imputation → standardisation → PCA to 64 components
→ random forest, 500 trees, `cnv_rf_conservative` configuration) over 632
genome-windowed CNV features. Each of the five patient-disjoint outer folds
exported per-feature impurity importances; `scripts/07_aggregate_cnv_importance.py`
aggregates them into a cross-validated ranking
(`lgd2_cnv_feature_importance_aggregated.csv`).

![Figure 1.5 — Cross-validated CNV feature importance (cnv_only random forest).](fig_cnv_feature_importance.png)

**Figure 1.5.** Top-20 CNV features by mean impurity importance across the five
outer folds (error bars = SD across folds). The highest-ranked features are
chromosome-arm–level gains/losses at **20p, 11q, 7p, 17p and 12q**, all present in
the top ranks of every fold (`n_folds = 5`); 17p and 7p coincide with the *TP53*
and *EGFR* loci, and 20p/20q gains are recurrent in oesophageal adenocarcinoma.
Two caveats temper this: (i) the importance spread is narrow — the top feature
(0.0135) is only ~1.15× the 20th (0.0118) — because the PCA step upstream of the
forest distributes correlated arm-level signal across components, so *relative
ranking and fold-to-fold stability are more informative than absolute magnitude*;
and (ii) impurity importance is a model-internal attribution, not a causal claim.
A window→gene annotation mapping is a natural next refinement.

## 1.7 Limitations

**[DRAFT]**
- Primary-metric benefit is not conclusive (ΔAUPRC CI includes zero); the
  chapter's claims are calibrated accordingly.
- Single-cohort, single-centre; **no external validation** — stated as a boundary,
  not a claim.
- Small positive class (50 progressor patients) limits power; CIs are wide.
- CNV importance is model-internal (impurity-based) and compressed by upstream
  PCA; it is descriptive, not causal (Section 1.6). Histology attention maps and
  the fusion case table remain pending a final-checkpoint cluster run.

## 1.8 Summary

**[DRAFT]** In a prespecified patient-level analysis of 150 Barrett's patients,
adding histopathology to copy-number features improved near-term LGD2+
progression prediction on ROC AUC and Brier score (CIs excluding zero) and on
AUPRC in point estimate (CI including zero). The most reliable combiner was simple
late-mean fusion; more complex fusion architectures did not improve on it. The
clinical trade-off is a substantial sensitivity gain at modest specificity cost.
